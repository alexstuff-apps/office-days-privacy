Office Days for Android

Privacy Policy

Effective 15 October 2026

Short version: Office Days keeps everything on your phone. It has no internet access, no accounts, no analytics and no ads. Nobody, including us, receives your data.

Who we are

Office Days is made by Alexandru Florian Ionescu, publishing as Alexstuff Apps. For any question about this policy or your data, write to alexstuff.apps+officedays@gmail.com.

What the app stores

To do its job, the app stores the following, only on your device:

  • the days you log as office days
  • any notes you add to those days
  • your attendance policy (work-from-home share and monthly cap)
  • holiday adjustments you make to a month's work days, and each finished month's quota
  • your theme choice (system, light or dark)

The app doesn't ask for your name, email, location or any other personal details.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no permission to use the internet, so it can't send anything anywhere. It contains no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking of any kind, and there is no account to sign in to. We never receive or see your data.

Sharing

We don't share data with anyone, because we don't have any. No third parties process data for this app.

Backups

Android's automatic cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for this app, so your data isn't copied to Google's servers or to another phone without you knowing.

Export and import

You can export your data to a file from Settings, for example to keep a copy or move to a new phone. This only happens when you ask for it, and you choose where the file goes. Once it's saved somewhere like Google Drive or your Downloads folder, the app no longer controls it, and the rules of that service or device apply. Importing reads a file you pick and stores its contents on your device.

How long data is kept, and how to delete it

Your data stays on your device until you remove it. You can:

  • tap a logged day to remove it, along with its note
  • clear the app's storage in Android Settings → Apps → Office Days → Storage
  • uninstall the app, which deletes everything it stored

Exported files are yours to keep or delete.

Who the app is for

Office Days is meant for working adults (18 and over). It isn't directed at children, and it doesn't knowingly handle children's data. In any case, the app doesn't collect anything.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this same address with a new effective date. If the app ever started collecting data, this policy would be updated first, and that would also show on the app's Google Play page.
